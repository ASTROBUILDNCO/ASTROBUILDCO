Government & Prime Contractor Support

Capability Statement.

AstroBuildCo is a turnkey telecommunications and critical-infrastructure field-services partner, supporting agencies, prime contractors, and infrastructure teams from scope coordination through field execution and response.

Core capabilities

  • Tower construction, modification, and decommissioning support
  • Wireless ISP and microwave equipment installation
  • Cabinet, shelter, grounding, and power-system integration
  • Inspection, preventative maintenance, and field support
  • Subcontracted scopes and emergency-response support

Turnkey delivery approach

AstroBuildCo works from scope coordination through site activity, field execution, and follow-through. The delivery model is designed for telecommunications construction, upgrades, maintenance, and response work.

We support agency and prime-contractor teams that need practical, dependable field-services capacity.

Who we support

  • Government agencies and public-sector infrastructure teams
  • Prime contractors and subcontracting partners
  • Commercial telecommunications and wireless providers
  • Critical-infrastructure owners and operators
